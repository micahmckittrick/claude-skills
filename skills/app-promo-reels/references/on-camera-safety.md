# On-Camera Safety

Promo reels are public and get copied, so anything on screen is effectively published. Check
every recording against this list, including lists, sidebars and the edges of the frame, not
just the part the caption points at.

## Keep off camera

| Category | Examples | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Real people | Usernames, gamertags, player or member lists, contact names, avatars, comments, leaderboards | Use fictional demo accounts. For apps that show public community data, record screens without member lists, or cut and zoom so they never appear in frame. |
| Personal data | Emails, phone numbers, addresses, account numbers, real transaction history | Seed fictional values; never record your own account. |
| Real businesses in sample data | Merchant names, banks, clinics, employers that might be real | Rename sample data to clearly fictional names ("Neighborhood Vet Clinic", "Everyday Checking"). |
| Nudity and sensitive imagery | Artwork in content libraries (for example classic tarot or fine-art images), medical images | Choose searches and items you've checked first. Keep a list of safe and unsafe items in `PRODUCTS.md`. |
| System UI | Permission prompts, notification banners, low-battery alerts, debug overlays, TestFlight banners | Grant permissions beforehand, turn on Do Not Disturb, use release-like builds, and cut before any prompt. |
| Secrets | API keys, tokens, internal URLs, admin screens | Never record developer or admin views. |
| Unreleased features | Anything behind a flag that isn't live | Show only what users can get today. |

## Claims and wording

- Captions and end cards describe what the product actually does today.
- Pricing must be honest: say what's free and what's paid ("Budget is part of the one-time
  Pro upgrade").
- Health, finance, legal or safety products need a short disclaimer in the end-card footer.
- When the product shows another company's game, data or brand, add a non-affiliation line
  ("Not affiliated with or endorsed by <company>").
- No fake reviews, ratings, download counts or testimonials.

## Rights

- Don't put commercial music in the rendered file. Add sound in the platform from its
  licensed library.
- Only use fonts, icons and images you're licensed to use in marketing.
- Get consent before showing anyone's real content, even a friend's.

## Last check before hand-off

Scrub through each rendered reel's contact sheet at a 0.5-second step and look specifically
for names, faces, dialogs and text you didn't intend to show. Re-cut the clip rather than
hoping nobody will notice.
